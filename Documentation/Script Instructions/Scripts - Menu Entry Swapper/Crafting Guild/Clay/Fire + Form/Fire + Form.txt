FIRE + FORM - MES
Script variant: Crafting Guild / Fire + Form (Menu Entry Swapper)

BEFORE YOU START

- You need level 40 Crafting to enter the Crafting Guild.

- Complete the Hard Falador achievements to unlock the Crafting Guild bank chest. The deposit box cannot load a preset.

- Prepare a bank preset containing soft clay. Load it manually once before starting; Menu Entry Swapper uses the last loaded preset when the script clicks the bank chest.

- Start beside the Crafting Guild bank chest. Keep the camera and game layout consistent when capturing coordinates and running the script.

STEPS TO RUN

1. In Setup > Coordinates, capture [Bank Prime] on the bank chest from your starting position.

2. Capture [Pottery Wheel] while standing at the bank chest.

3. At the pottery wheel, open the interface and capture [Pottery Menu] on the first menu option for your recipe.

4. Open that menu and capture [Pottery Sub-Menu] on the next option.

5. Open the sub-menu and capture [Item] on the pottery item you want to make.

6. Capture [Pottery Oven] while standing at the pottery wheel.

7. Capture [Bank Main] on the bank chest while standing at the pottery oven.

8. Check the Setup Checklist and resolve any Missing or Invalid entries before starting.

9. Press Start and choose how many runs to complete. Watch the first two inventories to confirm forming, firing, and banking work correctly.

ADDITIONAL SCRIPT NOTES

- The first inventory selects [Pottery Menu], [Pottery Sub-Menu], and [Item]. Later inventories press Space at the wheel to repeat the selected recipe.

- The script completes [Sleep Form] at the wheel, moves to the pottery oven, presses Space to fire, waits for [Sleep Fire], and returns to the bank.

- The bank chest loads your last used preset when clicked. Adjust the three independent walking timers and the forming and firing timers in Setup > Timer if needed.
