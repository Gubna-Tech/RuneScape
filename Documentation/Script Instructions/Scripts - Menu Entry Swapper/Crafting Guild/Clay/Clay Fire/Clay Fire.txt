CLAY FIRE - MES
Script variant: Crafting Guild / Clay Fire (Menu Entry Swapper)

BEFORE YOU START

- You need level 40 Crafting to enter the Crafting Guild.

- Complete the Hard Falador achievements to unlock the Crafting Guild bank chest. The deposit box cannot load a preset.

- Prepare a bank preset containing the unfired pottery you want to fire. Load it manually once before starting; Menu Entry Swapper uses the last loaded preset when the script clicks the bank chest.

- Start beside the Crafting Guild bank chest. Keep the camera and game layout consistent when capturing coordinates and running the script.

STEPS TO RUN

1. In Setup > Coordinates, capture [Bank Prime] on the bank chest from your starting position.

2. Capture [Pottery Oven] while standing at the bank chest.

3. Capture [Bank Main] on that same bank chest while standing at the pottery oven.

4. Check the Setup Checklist and resolve any Missing or Invalid entries before starting.

5. Press Start and choose how many runs to complete. Watch the first inventory to confirm firing and banking work correctly.

ADDITIONAL SCRIPT NOTES

- At the pottery oven, the script presses Space to begin firing, waits for [Sleep Fire], then returns to the bank.

- The bank chest loads your last used preset when clicked. [Sleep Fire] must allow the entire inventory to finish; adjust [Sleep Walk] and [Sleep Fire] in Setup > Timer if needed.
