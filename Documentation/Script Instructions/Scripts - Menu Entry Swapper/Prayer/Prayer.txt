PRAYER - MES
Script variant: Prayer (Menu Entry Swapper)

BEFORE YOU START

- IMPORTANT: Load the correct bank preset manually before starting. Menu Entry Swapper (MES) reuses your last loaded preset instead of pressing a Bank Preset hotkey.

- Prepare the ashes or bones you need and the correct bank preset.

- If using a Beast of Burden, prepare its pouch, familiar action-bar slot and matching preset items.

STEPS TO RUN

1. Put the bones or ashes you want to offer on your action bar. In Setup > Hotkeys, assign [Skillbar] to that slot.

2. Open Setup > Coordinates and capture [Bank] on the banker, chest or bank booth used on the return leg.

3. Check the Setup Checklist and resolve any Missing or Invalid entries before starting.

4. Press Start and choose how many runs you want to complete. Watch the first cycle to make sure the clicks and banking work correctly.

OPTIONAL SETTINGS

- [Powder of burials]: Enable or adjust powder usage only if using that item.

- [Beast of Burden]: Optional familiar supplies, hotkeys and timing; use only with a matching bank preset.

ADDITIONAL SCRIPT NOTES

- When using a Beast of Burden, include the familiar scroll and super restore in your bank preset.

- Timing: Keep the default Sleep values unless actions happen too early or too late. You can adjust them in Setup > Timer; test a full cycle after each change.
