GLASSBLOWING - MES
Script variant: Glassblowing (Menu Entry Swapper)

BEFORE YOU START

- IMPORTANT: Load the correct bank preset manually before starting. Menu Entry Swapper (MES) reuses your last loaded preset instead of pressing a Bank Preset hotkey.

- Have molten glass ready and set up your bank preset for the glass items you want to make.

STEPS TO RUN

1. Put the molten glass used for glassblowing on your action bar. In Setup > Hotkeys, assign [Skillbar] to that slot.

2. Open Setup > Coordinates and capture [Bank] on the banker, chest or bank booth used on the return leg.

3. Capture [Item] on the glass item you want to make in the glassblowing interface.

4. Check the Setup Checklist and resolve any Missing or Invalid entries before starting.

5. Press Start and choose how many runs you want to complete. Watch the first cycle to make sure the clicks and banking work correctly.

ADDITIONAL SCRIPT NOTES

- Timing: Keep the default Sleep values unless actions happen too early or too late. You can adjust them in Setup > Timer; test a full cycle after each change.
