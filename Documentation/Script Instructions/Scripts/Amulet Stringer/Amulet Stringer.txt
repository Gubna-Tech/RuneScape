AMULET STRINGER
Script variant: Amulet Stringer (Standard)

BEFORE YOU START

- Have unstrung amulets and balls of wool ready in your bank preset.

- Keep their inventory slots in a consistent position when capturing coordinates.

STEPS TO RUN

1. In Setup > Hotkeys, set [Bank Preset] to the in-game hotkey for the preset containing your supplies. The script presses this hotkey when banking.

2. Open Setup > Coordinates and capture [Bank] on the banker, chest or bank booth used on the return leg.

3. In the Coordinates list, select [Amulet], then right-click the unstrung amulet in your inventory to capture its position.

4. Select [Ball of Wool], then right-click a ball of wool in your inventory to capture its position.

5. Check the Setup Checklist and resolve any Missing or Invalid entries before starting.

6. Press Start and choose how many runs you want to complete. Watch the first cycle to make sure the clicks and banking work correctly.

ADDITIONAL SCRIPT NOTES

- Timing: Keep the default Sleep values unless actions happen too early or too late. You can adjust them in Setup > Timer; test a full cycle after each change.
