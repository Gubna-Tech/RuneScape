CLAY FIRE
Script variant: Crafting Guild / Clay Fire

BEFORE YOU START

- You need level 40 Crafting to enter the Crafting Guild.

- Complete the Hard Falador achievements to unlock the Crafting Guild bank chest. The deposit box cannot load bank presets.

- Prepare a bank preset containing the unfired pottery you want to fire.

- Start beside the Crafting Guild bank chest. Keep the camera and game layout consistent when capturing coordinates and running the script.

STEPS TO RUN

1. In Setup > Hotkeys, set [Bank Preset] to the in-game hotkey for your unfired pottery preset.

2. In Setup > Coordinates, capture [Bank Prime] on the bank chest from your starting position.

3. Capture [Pottery Oven] while standing at the bank chest.

4. Capture [Bank Main] on that same bank chest while standing at the pottery oven.

5. Check the Setup Checklist and resolve any Missing or Invalid entries before starting.

6. Press Start and choose how many runs to complete. Watch the first inventory to confirm firing and banking work correctly.

ADDITIONAL SCRIPT NOTES

- At the pottery oven, the script uses Space to begin firing, waits for [Sleep Fire], and returns to the bank. Make sure the oven interface is ready to fire your unfired pottery.

- Timing: [Sleep Fire] must allow the entire inventory to finish. Adjust [Sleep Walk] and [Sleep Fire] in Setup > Timer if needed; test a complete cycle after changes.
