FIRE + FORM
Script variant: Crafting Guild / Fire + Form

BEFORE YOU START

- You need level 40 Crafting to enter the Crafting Guild.

- Complete the Hard Falador achievements to unlock the Crafting Guild bank chest. The deposit box cannot load bank presets.

- Prepare a bank preset containing soft clay.

- Start beside the Crafting Guild bank chest. Keep the camera and game layout consistent when capturing coordinates and running the script.

STEPS TO RUN

1. In Setup > Hotkeys, set [Bank Preset] to the in-game hotkey for your soft clay preset.

2. In Setup > Coordinates, capture [Bank Prime] on the bank chest from your starting position.

3. Capture [Pottery Wheel] while standing at the bank chest.

4. At the pottery wheel, open the interface and capture [Pottery Menu] on the menu option for your recipe.

5. Open that menu and capture [Pottery Sub-Menu] on the next option.

6. Open the sub-menu and capture [Item] on the item you want to make.

7. Capture [Pottery Oven] while standing at the pottery wheel.

8. Capture [Bank Main] on the bank chest while standing at the pottery oven.

9. Check the Setup Checklist and resolve any Missing or Invalid entries before starting.

10. Press Start and choose how many runs to complete. Watch the first two inventories to confirm forming, firing, and banking work correctly.

ADDITIONAL SCRIPT NOTES

- The first inventory selects [Pottery Menu], [Pottery Sub-Menu], and [Item]. Later inventories use Space at the wheel to repeat the selected item.

- Each inventory is formed at the wheel, fired at the oven using Space, and returned to the bank after [Sleep Fire].

- Timing: Set [Sleep Walk Bank to Wheel], [Sleep Walk Wheel to Oven], and [Sleep Walk Oven to Bank] for their separate routes. [Sleep Form] and [Sleep Fire] must allow the full crafting and firing actions to finish. Adjust in Setup > Timer and test a complete cycle after changes.
