WATERFALL FISHING
Script variant: Waterfall Fishing (Standard)

BEFORE YOU START

- ALT1 IS REQUIRED FOR THIS SCRIPT TO WORK!

STEPS TO RUN

1. Open Alt1 AFK Warden and set its no-XP alert for Fishing to about 45 seconds. Wait for the warning to turn red before capturing its position and color.

2. Open Setup > Coordinates and capture [Pixel Coordinate] on the red Alt1 AFK Warden warning after it appears. Capture the warning itself, not a pixel in RuneScape.

3. Open Setup > Colors and capture [Red] from that warning while it is red. Do not capture its normal color.

4. Capture [Fishing Spot] as a rectangle covering the clickable part of the Waterfall fishing spot.

5. Check the Setup Checklist and resolve any Missing or Invalid entries before starting.

6. Press Start and choose how long you want to run. When the Alt1 warning turns red, check that the script clicks your saved fishing spot and resumes fishing.

ADDITIONAL SCRIPT NOTES

- Timing: Keep the default Sleep values unless actions happen too early or too late. You can adjust them in Setup > Timer; test a full cycle after each change.
