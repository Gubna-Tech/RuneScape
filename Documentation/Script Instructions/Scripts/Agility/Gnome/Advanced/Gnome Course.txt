GNOME COURSE
Script variant: Agility / Gnome / Advanced (Standard)

BEFORE YOU START

- Capture the obstacles in course order. For each one, stand on the tile you would use to click it after finishing the previous obstacle.

- Capture that target before moving on.

- Use your normal starting tile for the first obstacle, and keep the same camera angle and zoom throughout.

- FOR BEST RESULTS, USE NORTH-FACING CAMERA WITH THE TOP-DOWN VIEW.

- YOU MUST WEAR THE AGILITY SKILLCAPE TO RUN THIS SCRIPT OR 89 AGILITY!

- Begin in front of the log balance.

- Capture Prime here, then capture Main on the return approach after the barrier.

STEPS TO RUN

1. Open Setup > Coordinates and capture [Log balance prime]. Stand in front of the log balance for Prime. Prime is the opening approach; capture Main from the return approach later in the course.

2. Capture [Obstacle net] from its approach tile, before clicking it.

3. Capture [Tree branch] from its approach tile, before clicking it.

4. Capture [Tree] from its approach tile, before clicking it.

5. Capture [Signpost] from its approach tile, before clicking it.

6. Capture [Pole] from its approach tile, before clicking it.

7. Capture [Tile]. After the pole, target the far north-east tile before [Barrier]. The landing position varies, so place the area far enough north-east to reach that tile.

8. Capture [Barrier] from its approach tile, before clicking it.

9. Capture [Log balance main] on the return-side approach after the barrier, before clicking the log balance again. Prime and Main are the same target viewed from different tiles. Capture each position separately.

10. Return to the tile where you captured Prime, keep the same camera angle and zoom, and check that every obstacle shows Ready in the Setup Checklist.

11. Press Start and choose how many laps you want to run. Watch the first lap to make sure each obstacle is clicked correctly.

ADDITIONAL SCRIPT NOTES

- Timing: Keep the default Sleep values unless actions happen too early or too late. You can adjust them in Setup > Timer; test a full cycle after each change.
