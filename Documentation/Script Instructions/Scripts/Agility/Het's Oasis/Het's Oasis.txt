HET'S OASIS
Script variant: Agility / Het's Oasis (Standard)

BEFORE YOU START

- Capture the obstacles in course order. For each one, stand on the tile you would use to click it after finishing the previous obstacle.

- Capture that target before moving on.

- Use your normal starting tile for the first obstacle, and keep the same camera angle and zoom throughout.

- YOU MUST WEAR THE AGILITY SKILLCAPE TO RUN THIS SCRIPT!

- START THIS SCRIPT TO THE TILE WEST OF THE FALLEN PALM TREE IN THE MOST NORTH-WEST CORNER OF THE COURSE.

- In RuneScape use Settings > Gameplay > Camera > System > Camera Mode > Freedom.

- Face NORTH and zoom out so all obstacles remain visible without camera movement.

- Dismiss pets before starting so obstacle clicks remain unobstructed.

STEPS TO RUN

1. Open Setup > Coordinates and capture [Fallen Palm Tree Prime]. Stand on the tile WEST of the north-west fallen palm tree for Prime. Prime is the opening approach; capture Main from the return approach later in the course.

2. Capture [Fallen Palm Tree 1] from its approach tile, before clicking it.

3. Capture [Rope Ladder] from its approach tile, before clicking it.

4. Capture [Gap 1] from its approach tile, before clicking it.

5. Capture [Stone Pillar] from its approach tile, before clicking it.

6. Capture [Rock Wall] from its approach tile, before clicking it.

7. Capture [Fallen Palm Tree 2] from its approach tile, before clicking it.

8. Capture [Small Gap] from its approach tile, before clicking it.

9. Capture [Medium Gap] from its approach tile, before clicking it.

10. Capture [Fallen Palm Tree 3] from its approach tile, before clicking it.

11. Capture [Collapsed Walls] from its approach tile, before clicking it.

12. Capture [Large Rock 1] from its approach tile, before clicking it.

13. Capture [Ledge 1] from its approach tile, before clicking it.

14. Capture [Gap 2] from its approach tile, before clicking it.

15. Capture [Ledge 2] from its approach tile, before clicking it.

16. Capture [Large Rock 2] from its approach tile, before clicking it.

17. Capture [Fallen Palm Tree Main]. Capture Main from the course-return approach to the same fallen palm tree. Prime and Main are the same target viewed from different tiles. Capture each position separately.

18. Return to the tile where you captured Prime, keep the same camera angle and zoom, and check that every obstacle shows Ready in the Setup Checklist.

19. Press Start and choose how many laps you want to run. Watch the first lap to make sure each obstacle is clicked correctly.

ADDITIONAL SCRIPT NOTES

- Timing: Keep the default Sleep values unless actions happen too early or too late. You can adjust them in Setup > Timer; test a full cycle after each change.
