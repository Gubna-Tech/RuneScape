BURTHORPE
Script variant: Agility / Burthorpe (Standard)

BEFORE YOU START

- Capture the obstacles in course order. For each one, stand on the tile you would use to click it after finishing the previous obstacle.

- Capture that target before moving on.

- Use your normal starting tile for the first obstacle, and keep the same camera angle and zoom throughout.

- FOR BEST RESULTS, USE SOUTH-FACING CAMERA WITH THE TOP-DOWN VIEW.

- START THIS SCRIPT WHILST STANDING NEXT TO THE LOG BEAM.

STEPS TO RUN

1. Open Setup > Coordinates and capture [Log Beam Prime]. Stand beside the log beam for Prime.

2. Capture [Wall] from its approach tile, before clicking it.

3. Capture [Balancing ledge] from its approach tile, before clicking it.

4. Capture [Obstacle low wall] from its approach tile, before clicking it.

5. Capture [Rope swing] from its approach tile, before clicking it.

6. Capture [Monkey bars] from its approach tile, before clicking it.

7. Capture [Ledge] from its approach tile, before clicking it.

8. Capture [Log beam main] after descending from the [Ledge]. Use the landing tile after the ledge to capture Main. Prime and Main are the same target viewed from different tiles. Capture each position separately.

9. Return to the tile where you captured Prime, keep the same camera angle and zoom, and check that every obstacle shows Ready in the Setup Checklist.

10. Press Start and choose how many laps you want to run. Watch the first lap to make sure each obstacle is clicked correctly.

ADDITIONAL SCRIPT NOTES

- Timing: Keep the default Sleep values unless actions happen too early or too late. You can adjust them in Setup > Timer; test a full cycle after each change.
