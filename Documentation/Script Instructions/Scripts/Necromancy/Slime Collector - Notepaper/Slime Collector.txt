SLIME COLLECTOR
Script variant: Necromancy / Slime Collector - Notepaper (Standard)

BEFORE YOU START

- Keep magic notepaper and the other required supplies in your inventory.

- Magic notepaper is required for this variant.

STEPS TO RUN

1. Open Setup > Coordinates and capture [Pool of Slime] on the pool of slime you want the script to collect from.

2. Capture [Bucket of Slime] on the bucket of slime in your inventory.

3. Capture [Magic Notepaper] on the inventory slot containing magic/enchanted notepaper.

4. Check the Setup Checklist and resolve any Missing or Invalid entries before starting.

5. Press Start and choose how many runs you want to complete. Watch the first run and check that the intended actions happen correctly.

ADDITIONAL SCRIPT NOTES

- Timing: Keep the default Sleep values unless actions happen too early or too late. You can adjust them in Setup > Timer; test a full cycle after each change.
