CONTRACT BINDING
Script variant: Contract Binding (Standard)

BEFORE YOU START

- Go to the Magestix shop and the binding obelisk.

- Bring spirit shards, blue charms, and the pouches you need for binding.

- OPTIONAL: In the top-right of the Magestix shop, change "Item Display Mode" to "Icon Only". This can make it easier to locate Hellfire Metal and Blood of Orcus when capturing the Buy and Sell coordinates.

- Keep both available and the shop interface positioned so it does not cover the obelisk.

- KNOWN LIMITATION - Buying the last four or fewer shop items can fail, and selling the last item may shift another inventory slot.

- Monitor quantities rather than leaving a low-stock cycle unattended.

- DO NOT SELL ALL ITEMS FROM INVENTORY, LEAVE AT LEAST 1 OF EACH MATERIAL!

STEPS TO RUN

1. Open Setup > Coordinates and capture [Magestix] on Magestix, the shopkeeper.

2. Capture [Buy Tab] on the Buy tab in the open Magestix shop.

3. Capture [Sell Tab] on the Sell tab in the open Magestix shop.

4. Capture [Obelisk] on the obelisk while Magestix’s shop is open. Ensure the shop window does not cover the obelisk.

5. Capture [Hellfire Metal - Buy] on the matching material in the shop Buy view (not Sell).

6. Capture [Hellfire Metal - Sell] on the matching material in the shop Sell view (not Buy).

7. Capture [Blood of Orcus - Buy] on the matching material in the shop Buy view (not Sell).

8. Capture [Blood of Orcus - Sell] on the matching material in the shop Sell view (not Buy).

9. Check the Setup Checklist and resolve any Missing or Invalid entries before starting.

10. Press Start and choose how many runs you want to complete. Watch the first run and check that the intended actions happen correctly.

OPTIONAL SETTINGS

- [Offset - Buy]: Optional right-click menu adjustment; keep defaults unless the named option is missed.

- [Offset - Sell]: Optional right-click menu adjustment; keep defaults unless the named option is missed.

ADDITIONAL SCRIPT NOTES

- Timing: Keep the default Sleep values unless actions happen too early or too late. You can adjust them in Setup > Timer; test a full cycle after each change.
